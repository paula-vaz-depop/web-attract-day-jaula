# Team Day: Build Ticketmaster in a Day

Gigs are selling out, and every fan wants a seat. Build the ticketing API and the web app fans use to reserve seats.

- Fans pick an event, pick a seat, enter their email and reserve it.
- **Never leave a fan staring at a blank screen.**
- [REQUIREMENTS.md](REQUIREMENTS.md) is what Product wants. [TECHNICAL.md](TECHNICAL.md) has the technical rules.
- [seed.json](seed.json) has the events and seats.
- **There are no endpoints provided. Your team designs its own API**: the endpoints, what they take and return, and how errors come back. See [TECHNICAL.md](TECHNICAL.md).
- **One shared repo per team. Everyone commits to it from their own laptop, under their own name.** How you split the work is up to you, but every person's contribution should show up in the history.
- No payments: reserving is the whole flow.
- Requirements will change during the day. Be ready.
